Overview
In *Ocean Voyage*, you play as a young captain traveling between ocean kingdoms. Your goal is to earn money, recruit crew members, survive dangerous storms, and upgrade your ship until it reaches Level 8.

Available Commands:
#help
Displays a list of all available commands.

#status
Displays the player's current status, including:
* Money
* Food
* Water
* Crew size
* Ship level
* Current location

#fish
Catch fish to earn money.
* More crew members increase earnings.
* Fishing may trigger random events such as treasure discoveries or pirate attacks.

#sail [location]
Travel to another kingdom.
Traveling consumes food and water and may trigger storms.
Available locations:
* `M's Kingdom`
* `I's Kingdom`
* `A's Kingdom`

#shop
Opens the shop menu at locations that contain shops.
Inside the shop, players may:
1. Buy food
2. Buy water
3. Upgrade ship
4. Recruit crew members

#map
Displays the world map.
Map Layout:
        A's Kingdom
             |
             |
M's Kingdom --- I's Kingdom

#quit
Exits the game.


# SPOILER ALERT – Challenges & Win Conditions
#How to Win
Upgrade your ship to Level 8.

#How to Lose
The game ends if:
* Food reaches 0
* Water reaches 0

#Storms
Storms may occur while sailing.
Storms can reduce:
* Food
* Water
Upgrading your ship increases storm safety.

#Restricted Area
`A's Kingdom` cannot be accessed until the player's ship reaches Level 3.

#Random Events
Fishing may trigger:
* Treasure discoveries
* Pirate attacks
These events affect gameplay and create different outcomes each time the game is played.
